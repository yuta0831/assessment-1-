BHP

Mining Operations

  • Newman Operations: Major iron ore mining hub.
  • Jimblebar: Inland Pilbara mining operation.
  • Mining Area C: Iron ore hub incorporating the South Flank deposit.
  • Yandi: Established Pilbara iron ore mine

What BHP Mines

BHP mines essential commodities, primarily focusing on iron ore, copper, metallurgical (steelmaking) coal, and potash, along with associated byproducts like gold, silver, and uranium

Employees

BHP employs approximately 40,238 direct employees and over 80,000 total workers including contractors globally as of mid-2026
