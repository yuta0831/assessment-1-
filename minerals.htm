10 most mined minerals in Western Australia

  • Iron Ore:A rock mined extensively in the Pilbara region to make steel.
  • Gold:A precious yellow metal found in areas like Kalgoorlie's Golden Mile.
  • Bauxite:A sedimentary rock rich in aluminum oxide, primarily mined in the Pilbara region.
  • Nickel:A metallic element used in the production of stainless steel and other alloys.
  • Copper:A reddish-orange metal used in electrical wiring and plumbing.
  • Lead:A heavy, soft metal used in batteries and radiation shielding.
  • Zinc:A bluish-white metal used to coat steel to prevent rusting.
  • Diamonds:A precious gemstone found in the Kimberley region.
  • Salt:A mineral used for food seasoning and industrial applications.
  • Limestone:A sedimentary rock used in construction and cement production.
