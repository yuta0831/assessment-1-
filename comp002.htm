RIO TINTO

Mining Operations

Rio Tinto operates an integrated network of iron ore mines in the Pilbara region of Western Australia. Its operations include Tom Price, Paraburdoo, Gudai-Darri, Yandicoogina, West Angelas and other mining operations.

What RIO TINTO Mines

io Tinto mainly mines iron ore in Western Australia. Its Pilbara operations produce iron ore products including Pilbara Blend, which is used by steel producers.

Employees

Rio Tinto had a Western Australia workforce of 19,371 people
