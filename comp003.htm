Fortescue

Mining Operations

Fortescue operates several mining operations in the Pilbara region of Western Australia. Its operations include Christmas Creek, Cloudbreak, Eliwana, Solomon and Iron Bridge.

What Fortescue Mines

Fortescue mainly mines iron ore in Western Australia. Its Pilbara operations produce iron ore for customers around the world.

Employees

More than 20,000 people work across Fortescue's global team.
